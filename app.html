A calm UI framework in the Notion idiom – as web components, without a build step. This page is the living preview: sidebar, page tree, callouts, todos, toggles and database views in one shell.

Core idea: NotionKit supplies the look and the shell; the editor is mounted through <nk-block-host> (BlockNote, say), and the data comes from your own API.
